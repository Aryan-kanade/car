Round 8 — "Signature Experiences": research verdict — My Garage, weather-aware coaching, and a water-beading simulator are 5/5-unique gaps NO detailing brand fills (verified: Adam's and Chemical Guys have no vehicle personalization; nobody does rain-aware advice). Combined with client-side intelligence, award-craft motion, an account hub, and shop depth — ~25 new capabilities.

## Phase 1 — Signature experiences (the "only a detailing brand could do this" layer)

1. **🌊 Foam-wipe hero** — canvas foam overlay on the hero; visitors wipe it away with mouse/touch to reveal the headline (destination-out technique, ~60 lines; "Wipe to reveal" hint; auto-completes at 70%). THE signature first impression.
2. **🚗 My Garage** (`/garage`) — first-mover feature: add your cars (make, model, colour, paint condition: new/coated/swirled, parked: garage/street) → each car gets a matched routine from the quiz matrix → PDPs show "Recommended for your Garage" badges; localStorage-persisted; nav link + mega-menu entry
3. **🌦 Weather-aware Detailing Coach** — geolocation + Open-Meteo (free, no key): "Rain expected Thu — wax today" card on home; a wash-quality forecast (next 48h score: good/fair/poor windows); explicit opt-in for location, graceful fallback to Mumbai default
4. **💧 Hydrophobic Beading Simulator** — canvas panel where rain beads grow, merge (metaball-style) and sheet off; toggle "Untreated vs Ceramic-coated" to see the difference — a shareable, physical product demo no competitor has

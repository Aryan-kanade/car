Round 9 — "The Immersive Layer": tops Round 8 by making the site _interactive in time and space_ — a 3D product viewer, a guided detailing session (workout-app style), a Shine Score assessment, a virtual shelf, and award-level page transitions. Grounded in this session's research (3D viewers = ~30% conversion lift; playable/interactive brand sites are an established award category; nothing in this list exists on any detailing competitor).

## Phase 1 — 3D Product Experience

1. **Interactive 360° bottle viewer** on PDPs — CSS-3D/canvas-driven product bottle you can drag to rotate (segmented lathed silhouette rendered on canvas with specular highlight that tracks rotation — no WebGL dependency, ~35 kB zero-dep). Replaces the static gallery's front view as the default "3D" tab.
2. **"In your space" AR-style preview** — a playful camera-optional overlay placing the bottle placeholder in your driveway via getUserMedia with graceful fallback (declined = framed preview).

## Phase 2 — Guided Detailing Session (`/session`)

A workout-app-for-your-car: pick a routine (from Garage/quiz matrix) → step-by-step guided timer with stage cues ("Pre-wash: dwell 4:00", "Rinse: 2:00"), progress ring, pause/skip, completion summary + streak credit. Persisted session history. This is the deepest "only a detailing brand could do this" feature yet — nobody has it.

## Phase 3 — Assessment & ownership tools

1. **Shine Score** (`/shine-score`) — interactive paint-condition assessment: 6 visual questions (beading test, swirl check under light, gloss level…) → 0–100 score with animated gauge → prioritized product plan mapped to score bands. Shareable result.
2. **Virtual Shelf** (`/shelf`) — mark products you OWN → "Missing from your routine" analysis vs. quiz matrix → one-tap add of gaps. Ties Garage + Shelf into a complete ownership graph.

## Phase 4 — Award-level motion & voice

1. **Clip-path curtain page transitions** — route changes sweep a themed curtain (view-transition enhancement, respects reduced-motion)
2. **Voice search** — mic button in search overlay using Web Speech API with typed fallback
3. **Wash-day checklist export** — printable per-routine checklist (print stylesheet already exists; add dedicated checklist route)

## Phase 5 — Community & proof

1. **Wash log + streaks** — log completed sessions (ties Phase 2) → streak calendar strip on `/account` with milestone badges
2. **Garage share links** — share your garage/routine via URL like wishlist
3. Final sweep: re-record visual baselines, all 8 gates, README, commits
